# Financial Model App

This Streamlit app allows users to perform a comprehensive capital budgeting analysis. It includes features for:

- Depreciation scheduling (using the Declining Balance method)
- Loan repayment breakdown (Equal Principal Payments)
- Cash flow analysis including EBIT, Tax, and Earnings After Tax
- NPV (Net Present Value) and IRR (Internal Rate of Return) calculation
- Monte Carlo Simulation for NPV
- Smart AI-based suggestions for improvements

## Features

- **Capital Budgeting**: Input the cost of the equipment, shipping, installation, and salvage value to calculate depreciation.
- **Loan Amortization**: Input your loan amount, interest rate, and term to see the repayment breakdown.
- **Revenue & Costs**: Simulate annual revenue, operating costs, and corporate tax rate.
- **Interactive Charts**: View bar charts for Depreciation, Loan Repayments, and EBIT/Tax.
- **Monte Carlo Simulation**: Simulate a range of NPVs to better understand project viability under varying conditions.
